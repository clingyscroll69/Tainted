## Direction contract

THESIS: Depth is how far a stranger's data travels before it reaches something dangerous. The run is
a descent and the surface is one water column; it refuses the category's filterable severity table.

OWN-WORLD: Abyssal blue ground darkening by depth stage, chalk marine-snow particulate, hairline
panels lifted a value off the ground. Thermocline cyan is the only instrument accent. Warm red is
physically impossible at depth without your own light, so it appears **only** where an exploit was
executed; DEMONSTRATED is half-lit amber, REPORTED stays cold and unlit. Saira Condensed display,
Martian Mono instrument voice, Saira body. No badge or chip does the ranking; scale contrast does.

STORY: The developer reads the verdict at a glance, sees which findings the lamp actually lit versus
which are shapes in the dark, and arms the fix.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Three-column mission panel at 1440. Left rail: the descent — taint-path stages from
Surface through Thermocline (the ownership check) to the Mesophotic Wall of sinks, active stage ringed
cyan, footer carrying run time, deepest reach, ownership state. Centre: the water column, verdict set
in condensed display at full strength, the thermocline band ruling across it, two giant mono readouts
(PROVEN, REPORTED) low-left, Arm & Prove sitting on the band. Right stack: run target, active plane,
the three registers as a 3-up, proof log. Findings below as observations pinned at their depth.

SIGNATURE: **The lamp.** Arm & Prove ignites a finding from cold blue to lamp-red as the exploit
executes, unfolding its real executed steps, each step deep-linkable. Motion grammar: light and
descent only — nothing slides or bounces; reduced-motion resolves to final state instantly.
